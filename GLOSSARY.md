# Spindle

Spindle is a persistent story bible for fiction. These are the story-structure
terms its planning and drafting share; structure follows Robert McKee, and
prose-level craft (scene/sequel, MRU) follows Swain.

## Story structure

**Story Spine** (故事脊柱):
The one line of meaning a series or book is built on: its Central Question,
Controlling Idea, Counter-Idea, Spine Axis and ending type. A series has one
spine; each book has a sub-spine that answers part of the series question.
_Avoid_: Theme (a Theme is a secondary idea; a story has many), premise

**Central Question** (核心問題):
The question the inciting incident raises and the final climax answers.
_Avoid_: Major dramatic question, logline

**Controlling Idea** (主控思想):
A one-sentence claim the climax proves, written as a value plus its cause
("justice wins because…"), never as an open question.
_Avoid_: Moral, message

**Counter-Idea** (反主控思想):
The opposing claim the story argues against; the story is a debate between it
and the Controlling Idea.
_Avoid_: Antithesis

**Spine Axis** (脊柱價值軸):
The Value Axis whose Charge each Act climax turns.

**Act** (幕):
A run of Sequences that ends in a climax which turns the Spine Axis. An Act is
outline intent, not a story event.

**Sequence** (段落):
A group of Scenes inside an Act that ends in a scene with more impact than any
before it, answering part of the Act's question.
_Avoid_: Arc (an Arc belongs to a character or plot line)

**Scene** (場景):
Action through conflict, in roughly continuous time and place, that turns at
least one Value Axis by at least one perceptible degree. A scene that turns
nothing is a non-event.

**Main Plot** (主線):
The one plot line whose Value Axis is the Spine Axis.
_Avoid_: A-story, spine thread

**Subplot** (副線):
Any other plot line, with its own Value Axis and one structural job toward the
Controlling Idea: contradict it, resonate with it, set up the Main Plot, or
complicate the Main Plot.
_Avoid_: B-story, thread, side plot

**Convergence Point** (匯流點):
A planned place where a Subplot feeds the Main Plot; a Subplot's Sequence
climaxes land at or before one.

**Inaction Cost** (不行動代價):
What a character loses by doing nothing in a Scene; a Scene a character could
survive by inaction has too little pressure.

**Beat** (拍):
One exchange of action and reaction inside a Scene; in dialogue, one line used
as a tactic to get something.

## Values

**Value Axis** (價值軸):
A human quality that can swing between positive and negative, such as
trust/betrayal or freedom/slavery.

**Charge** (極性):
Where a Value Axis stands at a given moment, from positive through contrary and
contradictory to the negation of the negation.
_Avoid_: Polarity, state

**Value Turn** (價值翻轉):
A Scene's movement of one Value Axis from an opening Charge to a closing Charge.
A planned Value Turn is outline intent; a realized Value Turn is what the
written scene actually did.
_Avoid_: Value shift

**Gap** (鴻溝):
The difference between what a character expects an action to bring and what the
world returns; each Gap forces a riskier next action.

**Turning Beat** (轉折拍):
The Beat whose Gap turns the Scene's Value Axis.

## Characters

**Character Design** (角色設計):
A character's structural blueprint: contradictions, the four layers of self
(public, personal, private, hidden), conscious and unconscious desire, and the
pressure the character puts on the protagonist. It is separate from the
character's on-page voice and state.
_Avoid_: Character sheet, profile

**Character Effect** (人物效應):
What a Scene did to a character: reinforce, reveal, contradict, change, or
none. Change is earned only at a planned arc milestone; anywhere else it is a
character jump.
_Avoid_: Character development, growth

## Canon

**Key Fact** (關鍵事實):
A canon fact decided during planning because a turning point depends on it: a
death, a revealed secret, a relationship turn, or an item or power gained or
lost. It is bound to the planned Scene that must dramatise it.
_Avoid_: Planned fact (any planned fact; a Key Fact is the turning kind)

**Narrative Promise** (伏筆):
Something the story plants for the reader and later pays off, subverts or
abandons; its setup, reinforcements and payoff are planned against Scenes.
A Turning Beat that depends on information must pay off an earlier promise.
_Avoid_: Foreshadow, Chekhov's gun (both are kinds of promise), hook
